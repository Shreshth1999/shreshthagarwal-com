Background

I grew up in a business family in Sri Ganganagar, Rajasthan — real estate, agricultural trading, warehousing. My father runs M/s Sadhu Ram Sanjay Kumar, dealing in property and agri-stock through the Sri Ganganagar Dhan Mandi ecosystem. I started working inside the family operations early — running our jewelry showroom, handling dealer negotiations for our warehouse business, and watching closely how procurement actually works in India.

The pattern was always the same: opaque pricing, fragmented access, and decisions worth lakhs being made over phone calls and personal relationships. Good dealers lost business to whoever had better connections, and buyers had no way to know if they were getting a fair price.

That problem became Hub4Estate. Two years of research, pivots, and real deals later, I incorporated the company in March 2026. I'm currently studying at Mesa School of Business in Bangalore while building the company full-time.

Building
Hub4Estate
Founder · HUB4ESTATE LLP · Incorporated March 2026

A sealed-quote procurement platform. Buyers post a requirement — product, quantity, location. Verified dealers respond with their best price, sealed from each other. The buyer sees every quote side by side without knowing who sent what. Picks the best deal. The dealer is revealed only after selection.

No spam calls, no middlemen, genuine price competition. Starting with electrical materials — wires, switches, MCBs, lighting, fans, panels — and expanding from there. Currently operating with a concierge model: clients tell us what they need, we source the best price from dealers across India.

Building the platform, hiring sales teams across Hisar, Jaipur, and Delhi, and looking for a technical co-founder.
