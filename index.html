what I’m building right now.

Hub4Estate is the trust layer for how India buys.

Post a requirement. Verified dealers respond with sealed prices. They can’t see each other. You compare blind, pick the best, and the dealer is only revealed after you choose. No spam. No middlemen. No connections tax.
